This is a very simple module and one of the first I worked on. It just uses a micro-SD card slot, with pull up resistors on the slots protocol pins, as required, and 2 caps. I chose this because it means storage is easily replacable and 1 less thing for fallen to purchase as I already have micro-SD cards. This module is made to be placed at a certain position on the base board, where the main chip (T113 S3's hardware SD protocol is broken out. This module uses the simplest enclosure out of all my modules, literally just a box. But I am planning to put some sort of symbol on them, probably through stickers or a 3D print engraving.
